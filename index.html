how it works

From bare metal to a cloud your team can log in to.

01

Create your control plane

Sign up and get a dedicated control plane — its own state, its own certificate authority, its own accounts and roles. Not a shared panel with your name on it.

02

Attach your servers

One command per machine. Servers dial out to the control plane — no inbound ports, no VPN, NAT is fine. Commodity hardware is all it takes.

03

Hand your team a cloud

Projects, members and quotas, a full service catalog, an API and a CLI. Your developers self-serve; you keep the hardware and the data.

the arrangement

Built to be walked away from.

A managed control plane is a dependency. We designed it so it is never a hostage situation.

01Your workloads don't need us

The management link is outbound-only, and losing it degrades your site to self-managed — it never takes it down. There is no kill switch; the verb does not exist in the protocol.

02One tenant, one control plane

Your control plane is yours alone: separate state, separate CA, separate identity. Noisy neighbours have nowhere to live, and neither do cross-tenant bugs.

03Your hardware, your data

Compute, storage and traffic stay on machines you own, in racks you chose. We keep the control plane healthy, current and backed up — and that is all we hold.
